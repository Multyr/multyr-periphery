4. Queue scenario (historical fork, block 509 739 747)

This scenario starts from the state just before the adapter-cycle PDF, replays its first five transactions, then drives a full deposit → fee → withdrawal → payout cycle with the production worker classes. Fork-only setup: AUTO_HARVEST enabled on the FeeCollector, 1 bps instant-withdrawal cap, zero deposit lock, a fresh keeper wallet with KEEPER_ROLE, a 10 gwei local fee cap, and the USDC price feed's timestamp advanced for the 7-day jump (price, decimals and round IDs preserved). Why the earlier run reported a W4 failure

The test asserted that no fee claim was pending after step 10. The 7-day jump landed inside the Monday distribution window, so the same tick also ran step 11: it distributed the fee shares that CRYSTALLIZE had just minted and, under AUTO_HARVEST, queued a new claim for them. That is correct behaviour. The assertion has been changed to check that the original claim was settled in full, the USDC was distributed, nothing was left in the FeeCollector, and any claim still pending was queued in that same tick. W4 itself needed no change.

5. Issues and open items

Fix before go-live

  1. Gas estimate too low for try/catch operations (keeper). The estimate stops at the lowest gas where the outer call succeeds, which starves the inner call. W2 is the worse case: a failed POKE_APY still sets lastPokeTs, so it is not retried for 24 h. Fix: add a gas buffer (for example estimate × 1.5) or a per-worker gas limit.
  2. Follow-up loop re-sends a failed operation (keeper). After UpkeepPerformed(success=false), checkUpkeep stays true and W1 repeats the same failing op until VaultUpkeep backs off. Fix: stop the loop on an inner failure. A unit test (todo) reproduces this.
  3. VaultUpkeep griefing (core contract). Any address can send three performUpkeep calls with junk data and freeze VaultUpkeep for 1 800 s, repeatedly. Confirmed on the fork. Fix: re-validate performData against checkUpkeep and do not count mismatches as failures.

Review

  • Fee cap compares the full fee estimate including the priority tip. Arbitrum suggests a zero tip today, but a provider that suggests a non-zero tip would make the bot skip every transaction.
  • AUTO_HARVEST is not configured for the core share token on mainnet, so W4's harvestQueued cannot run until governance sets it.
  • liquidityStalenessSeconds is 0 on-chain; W5 falls back to 3 600 s.
  • Warm NAV reports nav = 0 with valid = true on mainnet; confirm this is expected.
  • Four of seven adapters are not direct APY poke targets for W2: Aave (covered by the rate push), Comet, Euler and Venus. Confirm the last three compute APY live.
